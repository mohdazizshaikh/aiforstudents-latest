Virtual Experiment Lab — first pilot (M16)

Educational prototype, not expert-reviewed or age/board-calibrated. Both experiments are original static browser simulations. They are not hardware laboratories or a replacement for teachers.

Fractions: compare equal-sized wholes

Use buttons to change two fractions between zero and one, including denominators from 2 to 8. Compare using cross-products. Try making two equivalent positive proper fractions with distinct denominators, such as 1/2 and 2/4. The colored strips have text equivalents, and the activities can be completed with a keyboard.

Two-switch conceptual series circuit

In our simplified model the lamp lights only when both series switches are closed. Try opening exactly one switch to interrupt the path. This model cannot predict every real electrical circuit. Do not use mains sockets, household wires, or real electricity for these experiments. An optional paper-only drawing is provided.

Progress, language and privacy

Practice checks are recorded as activity observations in this browser only. Storage is session-only by default, optionally remembered on this device, and cleared with the existing Clear local history control. It is not an intelligence test, mastery certification, exam predictor or career diagnosis. Hindi drafts and the science explanations await independent bilingual educator review.
