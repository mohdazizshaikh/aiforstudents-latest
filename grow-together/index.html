Three free cooperative game modes

01

Pattern Partners

One player gets the starting number; the other finds the step. Combine both clues to finish a number pattern.

02

Number Bridge

One player works out a shopping total; the other finds the saving. Work together on the final amount.

03

Story Signals

Each person receives a separate story clue, then the two clues are joined into a shared answer.

How does the game help?

It gives students an opportunity to explain clues, consider another perspective, and practice short educational puzzles together. After completing a round, the local activity history can show a team Proud Moment. That record reflects what happened in this game, not a psychological assessment of teamwork or intelligence.

Each mode contains 20 generated mission variants per provisional age band. A session shows up to three new missions before replay. Ages and difficulty have not been validated by developmental specialists.

Privacy and safety boundaries

There are no online opponents, public player accounts, friend invitations, contact uploads, third-party chat, voice calls or free-text messaging. The “encouragement” buttons display fixed text on the same device only. Player 1 and Player 2 are anonymous game roles—not verified identities. On-device progress is optional. This experience is not suitable for high-stakes career, IQ or personality conclusions.

Be mindful when passing a device to a younger child. The game is a prototype; age-appropriateness, language and accessibility reviews remain outstanding.
