Smart Learning Studio: learn, try, play, understand, grow

Two short original concept pilots cover equal-part fractions and a simplified low-voltage circuit-path model. Use buttons, check your response, read the explanation, and try an optional no-screen activity. A shared phone or keyboard can be used; individual laptops are not required.

What the two pilots teach

Fractions: 2 of 4 equal pieces represent the same portion as 1/2, if the whole and piece sizes are consistent.

Simple circuit path: a closed switch completes a simplified circuit pathway; an open switch interrupts it. This conceptual screen simulation is not a real wiring guide. Do not connect household electrical outlets or bare wires.
