# M12-A human review checklist (all tasks pending)

The machine-generated `CONTENT_REVIEW_QUEUE.jsonl` contains 3,200 current records. **No task should be considered approved until a qualified reviewer actually signs off.**

For each item record:

1. **Correctness:** Independently solve the task; check answer key, distractors, sequence dependencies, and assumptions. Try alternative plausible answers and note ambiguity.
2. **Hindi and English:** Verify same facts, units, conditions, difficulty, natural phrasing, and accessible reading level. Do not equate the numeric-token audit with bilingual correctness.
3. **Age suitability:** Check more than length and number size: instruction load, domain familiarity, suitable scaffolds, alternative difficulty, reading ability and neurodiversity access.
4. **Accessibility:** Keyboard-only play; spoken/linear descriptions of spatial symbols; screen reader and reduced-motion checks; low-end Android device usability.
5. **Safety and privacy:** No requests for names, school, addresses, sharing private work, unsafe physical experiments or risky peer interactions. Mark tasks with sensitive or unverified recommendations.
6. **Scoring fairness:** Do not equate scores across ages, languages, content versions, categories or access modes without evidence. Creative/social reflections must never be converted into intelligence/personality scores.
7. **Content workflow:** Record reviewer identity, date, language, approved/rejected status, rationale and specific revision; rerun automated tests on every changed item.

Do not assume an automated test pass equals an educational or psychometric validation.
