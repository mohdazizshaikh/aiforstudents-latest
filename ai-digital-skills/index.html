AI & Digital Skills Lab — first pilot

Original bilingual draft learning scenarios covering (1) AI patterns and limitations, (2) deterministic algorithms, (3) verification and misinformation, (4) digital privacy. Four experimental age bands and two distinct prompts per topic per band: 32 author-created checks.

What this is / यह क्या है?

यह अभ्यास AI को परखने, सही स्रोत खोजने और निजी जानकारी बचाने में मदद करने के लिए है। यह बुद्धिमत्ता, योग्यता या परीक्षा परिणाम नहीं मापता।

No live AI model is invoked, no personal identity is requested, no account or advertising tracking is needed, and no comments or messages are sent to strangers. Feedback is fixed instructional text, not an independent fact-checking service or verified safety certification.

For educators and parents

Talk through the explanations without requiring pupils to reveal private experiences. Encourage a trusted adult, an original reliable publication and respectful consent for social sharing. Age difficulty and Hindi drafts are not professionally approved; do not treat the prototype as curriculum-aligned.

Privacy and accessibility

Questions work with keyboard Tab, Enter or Space and are not timed. Local attempts share the site's optional browser-only persistence and Clear local history control. Repeated attempts are practice, not cumulative ability scores.

Important limitations

32/32 tasks await independent bilingual educator review. Online safety and current guidance can change; there is no real student usability, assistive technology or Hostinger HTTPS validation. Public child-oriented launch is NOT approved.
