    • Two Switches / दो स्विच — draw a conceptual circuit; **never** use real mains electricity

    Offline assignment packs / ऑफ़लाइन अभ्यास पैक

    Open the local learner card and five-step pack builder / अभ्यास कार्ड और पैक खोलें. Choose one of the two original lessons, English or Hindi, and single-card or full-pack mode. Print or use the browser’s Save as PDF option. No answer keys in learner output, student accounts or online submissions. The same builder offers computer-only lab activity sheets for every advanced draft model, with prediction, comparison and explanation spaces and source limitations. Educational and permission review remain pending; not a licensed public curriculum pack.

    Teacher lab launcher / शिक्षक प्रयोग चयन

    Choose an original draft simulation by subject in the existing Classroom mode. Each selection displays its limitations and opens an allowlisted local lab. No pupil records, grade mapping or verified lesson approval.

    Demonstration agenda / प्रदर्शन योजना

    Arrange up to eight draft concepts for the current tab. Reorder, remove or clear fixed model IDs. Normal opening keeps the agenda in memory; a reload or new tab does not carry it. This is not a saved lesson calendar or student record.

    Teacher facilitation — one screen, multiple voices

    Pick a lesson in the classroom mode; take the five steps: prepare, predict, explore, explain and apply. Rotate the generic roles Idea sharer, Reason checker and Question asker. A role is not linked to any name and the website does not collect student participation records.

    पाँच चरणों में काम करें: तैयारी, अनुमान, अभ्यास, समझ और कागज़ पर उपयोग। बोलने, सवाल पूछने और कारण जाँचने की भूमिकाएँ बदलते रहें।

    Accessibility and fallback

    Buttons work with keyboard/Tab and Enter/Space. Projector-friendly mode increases classroom lesson text. Printed worksheets provide a no-device option. The visual fraction and circuit simulations have text descriptions, but real TalkBack, NVDA and low-end Android testing is still pending.

    Privacy and safeguarding

    No pupil names, marks, attendance, student photos, free chat or class analytics are used in Teacher Mode. The main game's optional local progress remains a separate feature. Do not display individual learners' local history on a projector. Obtain appropriate school authorization before any real child research or pilot.
