# Free Educational Resources — Integration and Licensing Policy

**2026-11-23 | Planning + editorial policy.** No bulk import, scraping, licensing clearance or third-party API integration has been performed.

## Principle
We aim to help students discover **official and trustworthy free-to-access learning resources** from within original lessons. A resource being free to browse **does not** establish permission to download, redistribute, rehost, translate, screenshot, or edit it. For the zero-budget M15 pilot, **outbound links** are the safe default, not mirrors or embedded copies.

## Required resource metadata
For each resource link proposed for a lesson, record:
- Internal resource ID and linked concept/lesson ID; provider, exact URL, title and age/class suitability.
- Language, curriculum/board and textbook edition if *verified*; otherwise mark **UNVERIFIED**.
- Resource type and whether access is free *to view*; offline availability only if demonstrably available under the provider's rules.
- License/copyright holder and item-specific license terms; attribution notice if reusable. If license is unknown, **LINK ONLY**.
- Reviewer name/date for editorial suitability and last URL/access check.
- External-site notice that the third party's privacy practices and internet requirements may differ.
- Accessibility notes, low-data alternatives and a fallback if the resource is removed.
- Revision reminder and issue ticket for broken or changed destinations.

## Integration rules
1. Prefer our original short concept cards and interactive exercises, then a labelled **Open official resource** link.
2. Do not copy NCERT chapters, videos, scans, apps, brand marks, downloads or translations unless the *specific material* has reusable rights and attribution conditions are satisfied.
3. Do not automatically ingest, bulk crawl, cache or iframe a third-party site; check embed permission and child privacy first.
4. Do not record a child's external click history or refer identifiable information in the URL/query string.
5. No link shorteners, affiliate redirects, forced sign-up funnels, or claims of an official partnership.
6. Provide a text-based explanation and optional offline original activity if the linked provider needs data.
7. Remove or replace content on copyright/safety/link-rot complaint; record the change, reviewer and date.
8. Avoid unverifiable salary, exam cutoff, qualification, policy or career claims; external links do not by themselves validate a personalized recommendation.

## License review decision
- **LINK APPROVED:** exact URL is correct and content is suitable; no third-party reproduction.
- **COPY APPROVED:** item-specific permission/license verified, legally compatible, original source and required attribution stored.
- **HOLD:** suitability, destination, age, license or rights unclear.
- **REJECT:** prohibited, harmful, misleading or not appropriate.

Only a **qualified actual reviewer** may sign a decision. All initial proposed entries are **UNREVIEWED for lesson-specific integration**.

## Success check
M15 passes the resource linking gate only when both pilot lessons link to verified appropriate external resources, the links are clearly marked as external, accessibility and fallback are addressed, and no unlicensed materials are copied.
