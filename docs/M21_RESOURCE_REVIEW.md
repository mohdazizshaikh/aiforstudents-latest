## Decisions
1. **Keep both current pilot links at the existing provider-page level.** Do not replace them with unverified chapter/asset links or claim syllabus alignment from search snippets.
2. Do **not** mirror, rehost, embed, download, translate or cache NCERT/DIKSHA chapters or videos without item-specific rights.
3. The M15/M16 original draft concepts remain unreviewed, even if a linked provider page exists. Circuit exercises are diagram-only and explicitly not real wiring instructions.
4. External third-party privacy/network conditions must remain distinct from our static offline-first activities; links should be labeled as external.
5. Before a reviewed private teacher pilot, record the **exact** current board, class, book title, language, publication/edition, chapter ID, verified destination URL, classroom accessibility and responsible qualified reviewer decision. If the official topic-specific resource does not match, use the original lesson with an **unverified mapping** label and no implied certification.
6. Repeat accessibility/link checks on an actual authorized mobile/browser. A successful automated link check cannot certify educational accuracy, grade suitability or copyright permission.
