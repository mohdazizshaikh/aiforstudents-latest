# M34 — B2 adaptive practice calibration and anonymous classroom view (FP-076 / FP-077 / FP-078)

**Design-only, never a validated mastery system or a school dashboard.** All existing age bands and practice recommendations are provisional; content records are unreviewed. Do not turn practice count or scores into IQ, fixed aptitude, grades, career advice, intelligence, special-needs inference or a child profile.

## FP-076 Proposed pre-registration protocol

Prerequisites: independent subject/Hindi reviews for the exact source version, approved curriculum edition and target learning objectives, actual school/guardian consent and ethics/legal/safeguarding review, opt-out and withdrawal without penalty. Use original reviewed equivalent alternate items, not repeats of the training question. Establish independent pre/post/delayed objective-linked assessment, comparison design with qualified education researcher, preregister sampling and stopping rules, missingness/withdrawal handling and outcome criteria **before** collecting data. No student ID is generated by the prototype; research data handling requires a separately approved secure procedure.

Illustrative proposed thresholds for validation, **not implemented learner classification**: a trial would require at least three independent equivalent tasks per proposed concept across two occasions; trained educator checks conceptual explanation, item difficulty and translation parity; predeclared reliability/confidence intervals, false-positive/false-negative criteria and subgroup sample minima must be set by the independent statistician before recruitment. Do not compute a mastery label from these counts. If evidence is insufficient, contradictory, sparse or subgroup disparity is uncertain, no adaptive inference is permitted. Report aggregate uncertainty, effect sizes and missing data; avoid ranking or collecting protected traits through the app. Independent validation and replication are external FP-080, not automated tests.

## FP-077 Anonymous classroom-view contract

Prototype teacher screen may show only **lesson-flow state**: selected draft lesson, current step, total steps, generic rotating role, teacher-controlled question/answer reveal, and optionally an activity count such as queued lessons. Do not show individual correct/incorrect results, attendance, names, rosters, classroom identifiers, school, home details, grades, inferred proficiency, ranks or persistent student IDs. Group response metrics are **disabled** pending consent, privacy threat analysis and validation. No remote aggregation, account, database, analytics export or classroom synchronization. Teacher can reset or leave and hide answers; visible screen is intended for one local browser, not proof of who is using it.

Any future group summaries must be optional, anonymous and approved, with minimum group-size and suppression/differencing analysis, no small-cell reporting, no history linkage across sessions, no personal metrics or export. No claim of anonymity solely from a device or an age-band selection. Current specification deliberately reports no child outcome data.

## FP-078 Safe session boundaries

A browser tab, profile, shared phone or projector does **not identify a child**. One learner's session can remain visible to a subsequent user if the device is shared; do not infer personhood from tab storage, login-free use or age bands. Default history is in tab session storage; explicit browser opt-in can persist across visits until cleared. Turning remembering off revokes persistent saving while preserving current-tab practice; explicit clear requests reset active same-origin tabs without broadcasting responses. Another browser/profile/device and backups are outside this guarantee; suspended/closed pages can miss events. Use the shared-device handoff button/clear history, end the activity, close other open tabs, and use a separate browser profile or guest session for the next learner. A school operator must verify this on real devices. No background tracking, no child names or persistent cross-device identifier.

Printed learner cards contain source prompts only, no history and no teacher-only answers. Physical paper and screenshots cannot be remotely deleted. Teacher queue is transient; never treat queued cards, generic roles or teacher navigation as attendance or educational performance. Public child launch remains blocked by six human gates and qualified content review.
