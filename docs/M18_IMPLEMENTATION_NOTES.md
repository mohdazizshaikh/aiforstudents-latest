# M18 — AI & Digital Skills Lab, first coding pilot

**2026-11-23 | Status: CODE PILOT IMPLEMENTED; educational and child-facing release NOT approved.**

## Implemented and why
- Four original domains: AI pattern recognition and limitations, if/then algorithms and loops, misinformation/evidence literacy, and digital personal privacy.
- Four experimental age bands (6–10, 11–14, 15–17, 18+), two authored explanatory choice questions per topic/band: **32 question records**, all **humanReview=pending**.
- Local-first fixed feedback, two question tabs, accessible buttons, age and English/Hindi switching, optional paper reflections, no timed tasks or aptitude rankings.
- `digitalAttempts` stored via existing optional local browser history. Clearing local history also clears all digital attempts; a real participation may generate Digital Safety Explorer Proud Today evidence.
- Staging guide `/ai-digital-skills/`; app modules `assets/digital-engine.js` and `assets/digital-app.js`, no remote AI/chat, online user profiles, prompts, tracking or paid inference.
