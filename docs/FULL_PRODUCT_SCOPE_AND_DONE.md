## Definition of Done for the *full* envisioned platform

1. **Class 1–12 curriculum:** identify specific supported boards/grades/subjects/editions/chapters and publish an honest coverage matrix. Every claimed-covered chapter has original/licensed resources, verified answers, original bilingual lesson, accessible nonvisual equivalent, review trail and offline fallback. Class 1–12 does **not** mean all state-board syllabi until each board is separately mapped.
2. **Mathematics Lab:** interactive grade-appropriate number/arithmetic, fractions, geometry, algebra, graphs and statistics with deterministic reference cases, explanatory feedback, keyboard operation and qualified review.
3. **Physics, Chemistry, Biology and environment:** distinct accessible scientific models, explicit limitations, correct facts and safety instructions; no unsupervised mains wiring, dangerous chemicals or unsupported health/genetics conclusions.
4. **AI tutor:** if implemented, must be clearly distinguished from the current offline fixed-answer Digital Skills Lab. Use age-safe, sourced, moderated reviewed content with privacy and affordability constraints; an open generative tutor is **NOT live** and needs independent human safety/education/legal clearance.
5. **Adaptive learning:** learners control recommendations; on-device optional practice suggestions are not evidence of mastery. Any mastery claim requires independent study, bias/fairness checks, validated evidence and understandable explanations.
6. **Teacher planning:** accessible offline/projector lesson manager and original worksheets for shared-device classrooms without assuming one laptop per child. No identifiable school roster/attendance/analytics without a separately approved data system and permissions.
7. **Accessibility/free access:** Hindi and English, no compulsory paid API/account, keyboard/non-drag controls, screen-reader alternatives, zoom/reduced-motion, genuinely tested low-end Android, printed A4 and offline learning.
8. **Content/safeguarding:** qualified educator+Hindi review of publishable scope, lawful resource permissions, actual safeguarding/incident owners, India-appropriate legal/privacy assessment, ethical school permissions and independent decision log.
9. **Actual deployment:** authorized **restricted** Hostinger HTTPS staging, working CSP and access control (noindex alone is not authentication), source-asset hash verification, backup, rollback, real phone/PWA update, no sensitive release files online.
10. **Real learning evidence:** school/guardian-authorized feedback from genuine teachers/learners, minimal-data consent and documented usability and educational findings; no inflated efficacy claims.

## Mandatory status rules

- **PLANNED:** design only.
- **PROTOTYPE IMPLEMENTED:** working limited vertical slice; does not imply breadth.
- **ENGINEERING TESTED:** tests pass in a specified environment; not a human/field review.
- **CONTENT REVIEWED:** actual subject and Hindi reviewers approve exact versions/sources.
- **FIELD VERIFIED:** actual authorized classroom/host/physical device tested.
- **FEATURE COMPLETE:** frozen feature acceptance checklist plus required content/accessibility evidence satisfied.
- **PRODUCT COMPLETE:** agreed comprehensive subject/grade coverage and all feature completion criteria satisfied.
- **PUBLIC LAUNCH APPROVED:** separate real hosting, safeguarding, legal and educational release decision. **Currently false.**

## Work acceleration without bypassing safety

[The 100-item execution plan](MASTER_EXECUTION_PLAN.md) uses **2 × 50 tasks** as bulk planning units with CI/quality checkpoints every 10–15 items. Work can proceed concurrently across subject streams, but **human review and real hosting cannot be made true by code, mocks or checkboxes**. Completing 100 tasks will build meaningful breadth, yet full chapter-per-board content may need a larger follow-up item list after its actual edition/scope is verified.
