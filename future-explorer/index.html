FUTURE EXPLORER

Explore futures without being labeled

Students can browse original introductions to a broad range of fields such as design, engineering, writing, sustainability, public service, agriculture, teaching, data and more. Interest chips reorder a set of possibilities, but nothing is hidden based on test results.

Each field includes a safe optional offline mission with age-adjusted steps. Students themselves mark missions as explored; this is a journal of exploration, not a credential or competency badge.

Educational qualifications, licensing, pay, course admissions and career routes differ across places and change over time. Check official information rather than relying on a game. Useful external resources include NCERT Career Cards, National Career Service India, and O*NET OnLine (US occupations).

No personal game scores or identifiable child data are sent to these websites by the prototype.
