Study Lab: learn, recall, check

Our free local-browser starter Study Lab has eight short lesson themes across everyday maths, reading evidence, science inquiry, and study habits. Four experimental age bands provide 32 distinct short questions with English and Hindi wording.

How it works

Choose a subject, read a short tip, attempt an active recall question, check the explanation, and revisit a lesson after a gap if useful. Suggestions are voluntary; there are no streaks or score predictions. Your choices can be saved in this tab or, if you opt in, on this browser. Clear local history any time.

What it does not do

We have not mapped these examples to a board, examination syllabus, grades, disabilities or an official textbook. Qualified educator and bilingual content review are still pending. Results cannot predict exam marks, intellectual ability or future career suitability. For exam preparation, follow your school's official curriculum and a qualified teacher's guidance.

Privacy and accessibility

Answer buttons work with keyboard Tab and Enter or Space, with feedback announced to assistive technology. No student names, contact details, grades, accounts, network AI calls or trackers are used.
