GUIDANCE

For parents, guardians & educators

AI for Students is an educational practice prototype, not a diagnostic assessment, IQ measure, personality test, credential, admission decision or definitive career matching service. Age bands are experimental learning prompts, not a validated placement system.

What students can do

Try short puzzles and reflective activities; see feedback about actual attempts; create personal Proud Today reflections; explore different fields by self-selected interests; and use three cooperative same-device games with another person sitting nearby. Neither the solo nor cooperative game verifies that the person playing is who they say they are.

Safe boundaries

There are no online student accounts, stranger matchmaking, friend invitations, open messages, photos, voice or video chat in this build. Do not add personal details to creative notes. Paper, basic drawing and other harmless materials are sufficient for optional offline missions; a trusted adult should assess materials and supervise younger children.

Use alongside real support

Discuss what the child enjoyed and found challenging. Do not interpret one practice score as evidence of intelligence or future career success. Encourage healthy breaks and offline interests. Students may skip a day or decline any mission without penalty.

Public release for children requires bilingual educator review, accessibility testing, privacy and legal assessment, clear safeguarding procedures and appropriate consent policies if online social features are ever introduced.
